Project Overview
This project analyzes the financial performance of an early-stage startup using key financial KPIs. The analysis focuses on monthly revenue, expenses, burn rate, Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Lifetime Value (LTV), and cash run rate.

The goal is to transform financial and customer data into actionable insights that help evaluate startup growth, profitability, and financial sustainability.

Objectives
Analyze monthly revenue and expenses
Calculate the startup's monthly burn rate
Calculate Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
Estimate Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
Analyze the LTV:CAC ratio

Build an interactive financial KPI dashboard

Key KPIs
1. Monthly Revenue
Tracks the total revenue generated by the startup each month.

Formula:

Monthly Revenue = Sum of Revenue from All Customers

2. Burn Rate
Measures how much cash the startup is consuming each month.

Formula:

Net Burn = Monthly Expenses − Monthly Revenue

A positive net burn indicates that the company is spending more cash than it generates.

3. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
Measures the average cost of acquiring one new customer.

Formula:

CAC = Sales & Marketing Expenses ÷ Number of New Customers

4. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
Estimates the revenue or gross profit generated by a customer throughout their relationship with the startup.

A simplified calculation can be:

LTV = Average Revenue per Customer × Customer Lifetime

5. LTV:CAC Ratio
Compares the value generated by a customer with the cost of acquiring that customer.

Formula:

LTV:CAC = LTV ÷ CAC

A higher ratio generally indicates more efficient customer acquisition.

6. Runway
Estimates how long the startup can continue operating before its available cash is exhausted.

Formula:

Runway (Months) = Cash Balance ÷ Monthly Net Burn

Conclusion

By combining Excel financial modeling, Python analysis, and Tableau visualization, stakeholders can track critical KPIs and make data-driven decisions around growth, spending, customer acquisition, and cash management.
